# Provenance and attribution

This showcase was assembled from Tejas Ajay Rathod's local Mycelium research archive and submitted MSc thesis, **Assessing Out-of-the-Box and Fine-Tuned Geo-Localisation of Text-to-Image Models** (UCL / Cambridge Consultants, 2026).

## Source of truth

The submitted thesis supplies the reported protocol, conclusions and Tables 10, 13 and 14. Earlier experiment READMEs can contain planning states superseded by that submission. The source PDF itself is not bundled in this initial showcase.

The code is copied from a working tree containing uncommitted changes. See `code-provenance.json` for the archive commit, original paths and hashes of included files; do not assume the archive HEAD alone reconstructs these files.

## Third-party components

The project builds on FLUX.2 / Diffusers, PyTorch, LoRA, OpenStreetView-5M, GeoReasoner, PLONK, StreetCLIP, CLIP/OpenCLIP, DINOv2 and Grounding DINO. Third-party weights, datasets and repositories are not bundled. Their own licences and attribution requirements apply. Original paper references and implementation notes remain in the retained source and thesis.

No licence grant is asserted for the curated repository at this stage. A repository-wide licence should be chosen by the author after confirming applicable research and third-party terms.

## Authorship and interpretation

The README presents the author's work and thesis-supported findings. The supplied external review informed the strengths-focused framing; its recommendations and venue judgement are not presented as established results or endorsements. No human-participant study, clinical deployment or general-purpose geolocation benchmark victory is claimed.

## Presentation references

The author's `the-distribution-argument_offline.html` supplies the plausible / identifiable / representative framing and the visual explainability walkthrough. Selected embedded PNGs are copied unchanged, with hashes in `presentation-assets.json`. The full 63 MB slide deck is not bundled. Numerical mechanistic examples are attributed to their stated model and diagnostic sample, separately from the final E06 aggregate results.
